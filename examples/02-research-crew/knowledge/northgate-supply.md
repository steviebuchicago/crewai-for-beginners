# Supplier Brief — Northgate Supply Co.

*Fictional company. Synthetic data for the CrewAI examples.*

**Category:** Packaging and warehouse consumables
**Relationship since:** March 2019
**Contract:** NG-2024-11, renews 2026-03-31, auto-renew unless cancelled 90 days prior
**Annual spend:** $2.4M (our 2nd largest supplier by spend)

## Delivery performance

| Quarter | On-time delivery | Order accuracy | Avg lead time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 2025 Q1 | 96.2% | 99.1% | 6 days |
| 2025 Q2 | 94.8% | 98.7% | 7 days |
| 2025 Q3 | 91.4% | 97.2% | 9 days |
| 2025 Q4 | 88.1% | 96.8% | 11 days |

Four consecutive quarters of decline. The contract sets a 95% on-time floor,
which we have been below for two quarters without invoking the remedy clause.

## Pricing

Unit pricing rose 7.2% at the 2025 renewal against a category index of 3.1%.
Northgate attributed the gap to corrugate costs. Our own analysis put the
justifiable portion at roughly 4%.

## Incidents

- **2025-08-14** — Mislabelled pallet shipment sent to the Bristol site instead
  of Reading. 2 day production delay. Northgate credited $18,400.
- **2025-11-02** — Partial delivery on a peak-season order; 40% of units arrived
  six days late. No credit issued; dispute still open as of 2026-01-15.

## Concentration risk

Northgate is our **sole source** for three SKUs (corrugate inserts, anti-static
liners, pallet wrap). No qualified alternate supplier exists for the anti-static
liners, and qualification is estimated at 14-18 weeks.

## Account notes

Their long-standing account manager left in September 2025. Since then, response
times on escalations have moved from under 24 hours to roughly four days.
