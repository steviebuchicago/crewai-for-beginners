**Annual spend:** $4.1M (our largest supplier by spend)

Improving trend. They invested in route-planning software in Q2 2025 and the
gains held through peak season.

## Pricing

Rates are index-linked to diesel and reprice monthly. Effective cost rose 5.8%
across 2025, entirely explained by the index. No discretionary increases.

## Incidents

- **2025-03-11** — Warehouse stock count discrepancy of 1,240 units. Resolved as
  a scanning error on their side; stock was recovered, no loss.
- **2025-09-28** — Two-day service interruption from a driver strike at a
  subcontracted haulier. Ridgeline arranged alternate capacity within 36 hours.

## Concentration risk

**High, and structural.** Ridgeline handles 71% of our outbound freight and
operates the Reading warehouse under the same agreement. There is no warm
alternate. A transition is estimated at 6-9 months and would require running two
warehouses in parallel for part of that period.

## Account notes

The contract expired on 2025-12-31 and both sides have continued on the prior
terms. That means our **largest supplier**, holding **the highest concentration
risk** in the portfolio, currently has **no price protection and a 30-day
termination right**. Renewal negotiation was scheduled for October 2025 and has
slipped twice.
